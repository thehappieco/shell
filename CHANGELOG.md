# Changelog of @thehappieco/shell

Published to `github.com/thehappieco/shell` by `scripts/shell-package.sh`, which copies this file and refuses a version it does not name.

## 1.6.0 (2026-10-26)

The owner's new layout (2026-10-23; contract 1.2, 1.3, 3.2 and 3.3): the person moves from the side menu's foot to an avatar at the header's right, with a menu of their own. Additive for a host's code: every 1.5.0 call still builds.

- **The side menu's groups:** Espaço, Apps, then **Minha conta** (in that order), and no foot. Minha conta holds **Perfil** (the answer's `menu.profile`, `/account`, its first item), the answer's `menu.account` (since this release **Minhas compras**, `/account/purchases`, then Contatos and Integrações com IA) and **Segurança**, id.'s account page (the password, the passkeys and the recovery code), from the new member `account_security_url`. The group was "Da conta" ("Your account", "De la cuenta"); its words are now "Minha conta", "My account", "Mi cuenta". New icons for the three: a person, a receipt and a shield.
- **The header's avatar.** At the header's right, after the theme toggle and the language menu (a host with both handlers) and the host's `header-actions`: a circle with the person's initials and, at 900 px and wider, their display name. It is a WAI-ARIA menu button (`aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` while open, as `ThcLanguageMenu`): Enter, Space or ArrowDown open it on its first item, ArrowUp on its last, a click on its first; ArrowDown and ArrowUp go round, Home and End, ArrowRight and ArrowLeft inside a group; Enter or Space follow a link; Escape closes it back to the button and goes no further; Tab closes it; a click outside, focus leaving, a link, another page and the window crossing 900 px close it. Its menu (`data-test="shell-avatar-menu"`, `role="menu"`) holds the person (the circle and the name, not interactive), the Minha conta group's links (`role="menuitem"`, `data-id` as in the side menu), "Aparência e idioma" for a host without both handlers, a separator, and **"Sair"** in the danger color (`tokens.css`'s `--thc-danger`, on `--thc-danger-bg` when pointed or focused). The button is highlighted on a page of Minha conta.
- **The initials:** the display name in NFC, then the first letter of its first word and of its last word (one letter for a one-word name), each upper-cased in the person's language; no display name, or no letter in it, draws a neutral person glyph. Text only.
- **Under 900 px:** the top bar is the menu button, the h1, the host's actions and the avatar; the drawer holds the same three groups. The 1.3.0 account panel (a disclosure with the profile, the workspace shown, the theme and the language) is gone: for a host with both handlers the avatar's menu holds the theme and the language instead, as one row of the two icon buttons the header shows from 900 px: the theme toggle, whose click (or Enter, or Space) moves to the next mode in place and keeps the menu open, then the language's flag. The flag closes the menu and opens the language's choice in the middle of the screen, a modal `<dialog>` (`showModal()`, as the drawer) titled "Idioma", listing every language with its flag and its name in itself, the one shown marked `aria-current`; its list scrolls on its own within a capped height, so more languages fit. It opens with focus on the language shown; the arrows, Home and End move; Enter, Space or a click choose, which closes it and asks the host through `changeLocale` (so the 1.5.0 crossfade); Escape, a click on the backdrop and its close button close it with no change; focus then goes back to the avatar's button. The workspace is the drawer's selector alone.
- **Without data** (loading, signed out, unavailable, another person's console) there is no avatar: nothing of the person. **With the kept menu read-only** (1.4.0) the avatar's menu opens, its links follow nothing, and "Sair" says `aria-disabled="true"`.
- **"Sair"** still runs `beforeSignOut`, then follows the link to the console's `/signout`. New optional prop **`signOut?: () => void`**: a host that ends the session itself (the console) passes it, and "Sair" runs it after `beforeSignOut` instead of following the link. The `menu-footer` slot is gone (the side menu has no foot); only the console used it.
- **The data** (`GET /v1/shell`, still `v: 1`; members only added): `menu.account` gains `{"id": "purchases", "path": "/account/purchases"}` first; `menu.profile`'s title is now Perfil / Profile / Perfil (a 1.4 or 1.5 shell shows it under the name at its foot, where it reads as well); and the new top-level `account_security_url`, id.'s account page, which `parseShell` keeps only as exactly a plain web address (https, or http on a development `*.localhost` name; no credentials, query or fragment) and otherwise reads as `null`, dropping Segurança alone. `ShellData` gains the optional `account_security_url?: string | null`.
- `data-test` changes: `shell-profile`, `shell-profile-name`, `shell-account`, `shell-account-button`, `shell-account-panel` and its rows are gone; the avatar is `shell-avatar` / `shell-avatar-button` / `shell-avatar-name`, its menu `shell-avatar-menu` with `shell-avatar-head`, `shell-avatar-item` (each with its `data-id`), `shell-appearance`, `shell-avatar-prefs` (the row) with `shell-avatar-theme` (the toggle) and `shell-avatar-language` (the flag), and `shell-sign-out`; the language's dialog is `shell-language-dialog`, with `shell-language-title`, `shell-language-close` and `shell-language-list` of `language-option` (each with its `data-value`). The header's own `shell-appearance` link is gone: it is the avatar menu's item.
- **For an app:** bump the pin and pass nothing new (`beforeSignOut` as with 1.4.0; no `signOut`). On your screens: the side menu's order is Espaço, Apps, Minha conta, with Minhas compras and Segurança, and no foot; "Sair", the person's name and "Aparência e idioma" (which leaves your header) are in the avatar's menu at the header's right, at every width. Update your own tests that click `shell-sign-out` or read `shell-profile`, `shell-account-*` or the header's `shell-appearance`: open `shell-avatar-button` first. Speculation rules that list the console's pages (contract 2.4) gain `/account/purchases`, as fakeproduct's do. A Wappie or Mailie still on 1.4 or 1.5 keeps drawing the old layout from the same answer, with Minhas compras under "Da conta" and "Perfil" at the foot.

## 1.5.0 (2026-10-23)

The look's crossfade, and links without the look (decision 0052; contract 3.3). Additive for a host's code: every 1.4.1 call still builds.

- **New exports: `lookChanger(shown, doc?)` and `changeLook(update, doc?)`** (`motion.ts`, with `LOOK_CHANGING` and the types `LookChanger`, `LookDocument` and `LookValues`). `changeLook` is the crossfade: the browser's View Transitions API takes a picture of the page, `update` runs, and the picture fades into the new page over 0.3 s with thehappie.co's easing (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1)`). It waits for Vue's next render inside the transition, so a language's new words are in the new picture. It runs `update` exactly once, always: at once, with no crossfade, where the browser has no View Transitions API, under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, on a hidden page and on a page being prerendered. Inside the crossfade `update` runs a frame after the call. It starts no timer, stores nothing and sets no style.
- **Show every change of the look through one `lookChanger`.** `const look = lookChanger(() => ({ locale, theme }))` (what the page shows now), then `look.show({ theme: next }, () => yourApply(next))` for every change: the header's toggle and menu, My account, a save's answer and its refusal's put-back, and the account's look arriving (a session, `/v1/shell`'s answer). A change that differs from what the page shows starts a crossfade; one that does not runs at once, so the page still remembers what it shows. While a crossfade's update has not run yet (the frame the browser takes its picture in), every further change joins that update, after the ones before it: none runs ahead of an older one (a page turned hidden, reduced motion), none is dropped (its writes still happen), and an echo starts no second crossfade. `look.settled()` resolves once every change shown so far has run, before reading the look the page went to. Calling `changeLook` directly and comparing with what is on screen loses a put-back or an account's look that arrives within that frame. Never call either at boot.
- **tokens.css** gains the crossfade's timing, scoped to `:root.thc-look-changing`, the class `changeLook` puts on `<html>` while it runs, so no other view transition of a host takes it; none under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Links carry no look** (decision 0052, which supersedes 0050's links): `consoleHomeURL` is the console's root with no query, and `signInURL` carries no theme (it keeps `locale`, for the console's own error pages). Both still take the 1.4.1 arguments, unused.
- **For an app:** show your theme and language changes (your header's handlers, and where the account's look applies from `/v1/shell`) through one `lookChanger`, and give your first frame the company cookies' look before anything paints: a blocking classic script in `<head>` that sets `data-theme` (the site's `boot.js` pattern), or your server writing `data-theme` and `<meta name="color-scheme">` into the page from the cookies, as id. and the console do.

## 1.4.1 (2026-10-23)

The language and theme travel with the shell's links drawn without the console's data (decision 0050). Additive: a host that passes no `theme` gets the same links as with 1.4.0, plus the language on the console's home.

- **Signed out:** the notice's sign-in link to the console's `/auth/login` carries the host's `theme` beside `locale`, which the console sends on to id.
- **The console not answering, or another account's:** the notice's link and the brand go to the console's home with `?ui_locales=` and, when the host passes one, `&ui_theme=`, which the console shows for that page.
- **New export:** `consoleHomeURL(consoleOrigin, locale, theme?)`. `signInURL` takes an optional fourth argument, the theme.
- **For an app:** pass `theme` to `ThcShell` (the theme the page shows), as the console does. With the console's data, nothing changes: both ends take the account's language and theme from it.

## 1.4.0 (2026-10-19)

Moving between the console and an app without the menu reloading (design `docs/design/2026-10-12/shell-1.4-instant-moves.md`, decision 0047; contract 1.5, 2.4, 3.2 and 3.3). Additive: a host that passes none of the new props draws exactly as with 1.3.0.

- **The kept answer.** `ThcShell` takes `initial` (`ShellData | null`): the last answer of `GET /v1/shell` the host showed, which it keeps itself. While `result` is `null` the shell draws it as it draws data (mode `kept`, `data-shell="kept"`), so a new page has its menu and header in its first frame and only the content area waits; the fresh `result` replaces it in place, under the same brand, and a refusal draws what 1.3.0 draws. It is drawn only when its `console_origin` is the host's `consoleOrigin` and, when `subject` is given, its `sub` is `subject`; a host that passes a `fallback` (an app) gets it drawn only with such a `subject`, that is once its own session is known for the same person. An app passes its fresh `result` only once its own session is known, together with its `subject`: before that the shell cannot tell another person's console from the app's person. It speaks the host's `locale`, and the header's controls show the host's `theme` and `locale`: an app sets its language and theme from the kept answer before passing it.
- **Read-only.** `readOnly` draws the kept answer read-only (the console, until its fresh answer arrives): every link keeps its `href`, but a click follows nothing and shows no loading mark; the workspace selector says `aria-disabled="true"` and does not open; the root has `data-read-only="true"`, and the side column, the drawer's panel and the tabs row `aria-busy="true"`. The theme and the language keep working. A host's own `menu-footer` is the host's to disable.
- **"Sair"'s hook.** `beforeSignOut` runs, synchronously, when the person follows the shell's own "Sair" (not a host's footer, and not while read-only), before the browser follows it: an app erases its kept answer there.
- **New exports**, which touch no storage: `KEPT_SHELL_KEY` (`thehappie-shell.last`, the one key on the console and every app), `MAX_KEPT_TEXT`, `keptText(data)` (the JSON of `parseShell`'s checked copy, or `null`: keep nothing) and `readKept(text, consoleOrigin)` (back through `parseShell` with the host's console origin, or `null`; it never throws); and, for a page the browser prerenders, `isPrerendering`, `whenActivated(fn)` (at once on a shown page, else once at `prerenderingchange`, in order) and `activated()`, with the type `ActivationDocument`.
- **The shell still stores nothing.** The host reads, writes and erases the kept answer (decision 0047's rules: contract 1.5): an app keeps it for its own session's person, erases it at its own sign-out, at "Sair" (`beforeSignOut`), when its session has ended or is another account's than the kept answer's (as soon as the session answers, whichever of the two answers first), and at a console 401 or another account's answer. On a page the browser prerenders it writes nothing of what it read before the person arrived (it asks again then, and keeps that answer), and erases through `whenActivated`.
- **Prerendering is the host's** (contract 2.4): an app's `/console/*` documents name speculation rules for the console's pages by the `Speculation-Rules` header (a JSON file served as `application/speculationrules+json`), opt in with `Supports-Loading-Mode: credentialed-prerender` only when the request's `Referer` origin is the console's, are `Cache-Control: no-store`, and keep every boot side effect behind `whenActivated`, asking again for their session and `GET /v1/shell` when the person arrives.
- Update an app's own tests: the guard that no storage write carries the sub, the name or a workspace allows exactly `KEPT_SHELL_KEY`, written by `keptText`.

## 1.3.0 (2026-10-11)

- **No breadcrumb.** The header draws the `h1` alone; the `h1` and the menu's current link say where the person is. The words `breadcrumb` ("Você está em") are gone from `SHELL_TEXT`; nothing else changes for a host.
- **The title draws no focus ring.** It still takes focus by script at each page (`tabindex="-1"`, for screen readers); no `:focus` or `:focus-visible` outline is drawn on it. A host's own page titles that take scripted focus should do the same (the console's and id.'s do).
- **An app's tabs scroll sideways.** One row that scrolls when it overflows (touch, trackpad, Tab), and only the row: no scrollbar, an edge fade where the browser supports scroll-driven animations, and the current tab scrolled into view when the shell is drawn and when it changes.
- **Under 900 px, an account button.** The header's theme toggle, language menu and "Aparência e idioma" are drawn at 900 px and wider only. Below, the person's avatar at the right is a button that opens a panel: the profile (to Minha conta), the workspace shown (to every workspace, or "Escolher espaço"), then "Tema" and "Idioma" for a host that passes both `changeTheme` and `changeLocale`, or "Aparência e idioma" for any other. Escape, a click outside, focus leaving, a link or another page close it. Without data it shows nothing of the console's person: the theme and the language alone for a host with both handlers, else no button. The header's `data-test="shell-head-profile"` link is gone: the button is `shell-account-button`, its panel `shell-account-panel`. At 900 px and wider nothing changes.
- **A loading mark while the browser follows a link.** When the person follows one of the shell's links that the browser navigates (not one the host routes, a new tab, a modified click or a prevented one), the content area shows a spinner and "Carregando…" (a `role="status"` region; the page stays mounted, hidden, with `aria-busy="true"`) until the document goes; the menu and the header stay. A page restored from the back/forward cache drops it. The shell adds two window listeners for it, the click's end and `pageshow`, both removed on unmount. An app whose screen can refuse to leave (`beforeunload`) keeps the mark until the next navigation.
- `ThcLanguageMenu` gains `variant="list"`: the three languages at once as a radio group (one tab stop, the arrow keys ask for the next), the account panel's. Additive.
- "Todos os espaços" in the workspace selector has `aria-current="page"` on the workspaces' page.
- New words in `SHELL_TEXT`: `loading` ("Carregando…", "Loading…", "Cargando…").
- Update an app's own tests that read `shell-crumb` or `shell-head-profile`.
